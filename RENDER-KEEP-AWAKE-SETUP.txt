STRATIC MARKET — CRON-JOB.ORG KEEP-AWAKE SETUP
================================================

The app exposes a lightweight health-check endpoint:
  GET /health

After deploying to Render, create a job at https://console.cron-job.org/:
  URL: https://YOUR-RENDER-SERVICE.onrender.com/health
  Method: GET
  Schedule: every 5 minutes (or the shortest interval allowed by your plan)
  Expected result: HTTP 200 with JSON status "ok"

Replace YOUR-RENDER-SERVICE with the exact hostname shown in your Render dashboard.
Run the job once manually and confirm a successful HTTP 200 response.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS:
- This is an external health ping, not a guaranteed 24/7 uptime mechanism.
- Render may still restart, deploy, suspend or spin down a service according to its plan and policies.
- It cannot prevent crashes, database outages, deployment failures or maintenance.
- Use a paid always-on hosting plan for a production uptime commitment.
- Keep /health lightweight; do not add database writes or sensitive information to it.
