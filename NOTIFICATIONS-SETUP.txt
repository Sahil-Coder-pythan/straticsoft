Chat notifications (new in v8)
1. Deploy normally. Render runs "npm install", which now installs the "web-push" package.
2. No keys or accounts needed: the VAPID keys are created automatically and saved in your database.
   (Optional: set VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY / VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY in Render to pin your own.)
3. Open the site on the phone, sign in, wait a few seconds: a gold card asks "Turn on message alerts". Tap "Turn on" and Allow.
4. Needs https (your Render link is https) and Chrome on Android. On iPhone it works only after "Add to Home Screen" (iOS 16.4+).
Behaviour
 - App open and visible: a gold banner drops from the top; the red number does NOT increase.
 - App closed / phone locked / app in background: a phone notification arrives and the red number counts the messages.
 - Opening that chat clears the red number; the next messages start again from 1.
 - Notification has a Reply box: reply without opening the app.
