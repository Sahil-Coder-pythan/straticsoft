Important Android/browser limitation:
A website cannot force-launch a particular OEM Gallery app (for example Vivo Gallery) or bypass Android's system picker. The browser's <input type=file accept="image/*"> invokes Android's registered photo picker. Which picker UI appears is controlled by Android/browser/device settings. A guaranteed direct launch of the manufacturer's Gallery app requires a native Android app or a native WebView bridge.
