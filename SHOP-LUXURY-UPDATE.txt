Stratic Market shop-link enhancement

Changes:
- Adds a Profile photo option in the account menu (above Help). It opens the device image picker, compresses the selected image, and saves it to the signed-in account.
- Shows the saved seller photo in the public shop hero while preserving the existing seller name, link format, product cards, and app navigation.
- Adds a luxury-styled audience insights section to the owner's public shop dashboard: account-country view breakdown, signed-in named viewers with profile photo/name/product/time, and shoppers who liked products.
- Adds COD and online-order breakdowns to the shop overview.
- Analytics only reports identities for signed-in users. Signed-out visitors remain anonymous; their names and country are not guessed. Country is based on account-provided country, not precise geolocation.
